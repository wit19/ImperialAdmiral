Generated by harness hop S-B (`tools/section_census.py`; rerun
after any mission corpus change). Counts ONLY what the shipped `SagaMissions/*.json` say - no memory, no estimate.

- Example CORE1: 4 sections (start + 3 invisible).
- Invisible-until-activated sections per mission: min 0, max 22 - these are exactly the sections SectionActivated watches in the feed.
- Placeholder sections with NULL-GUID in the corpus: 0 - the feed's diff machine treats every GUID the same, these included.

## Why this settles the module's size class

The section space has as many nodes per mission as the mission carries sections: bulk 1-6 nodes (133/138), two outlier authors reach 23 (EMPIRE3) and 15 (OTHER24). The SectionGraph therefore stays a graph below 25 nodes - BFS distance and mask are cheap operations, and the basic-law check (passable, floor, no closed-status) passes in every case. Single-section missions are legitimate: the section space is deliberately silent there (one section = no distinction), and masks stay truthful at one candidate.
